# Nexus Shopping Centre – Data Analysis Project

## Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data from Nexus Shopping Centre to understand customer behavior, sales trends, and business performance. The goal was to extract meaningful insights that can help in decision-making and improving overall sales strategy.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains order-level information including:

* Order details (Order ID, Date, Status)
* Customer information (Customer ID, Gender, Age group)
* Sales data (Amount, Quantity)
* Product categories
* Sales channels (Amazon, Flipkart, etc.)
* Location data (State, Postal code)

---

## Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales vs Orders

Analyzed the relationship between number of orders and total sales to identify overall performance trends.

### 2. Gender-wise Analysis

Compared purchasing behavior between men and women to understand target customer segments.

### 3. Order Status

Studied order completion, cancellations, and returns to evaluate operational efficiency.

### 4. Top Performing States

Identified top 5 states contributing the highest sales to focus on high-performing regions.

### 5. Age & Gender Insights

Combined age group and gender data to understand which customer segments drive maximum revenue.

### 6. Sales Channels

Analyzed different platforms (like Amazon, Flipkart, etc.) to see which channel contributes most to sales.

---

## Key Insights

* Women customers contribute a higher share of total purchases compared to men.
* Certain states consistently generate more revenue, indicating strong regional demand.
* Most sales come through a few dominant online channels.
* Adult age group (especially mid-age customers) forms the core customer base.
* Order cancellations and returns exist but are relatively controlled.

This analysis helps in understanding customer demographics, preferred sales channels, and regional performance. These insights can be used to:
